3. Five Viral Marketing Hacks for the Tunisian Market
Traditional ads are expensive. Use these organic strategies to acquire your first 15 agencies.

Hack 1: The "WhatsApp Hand-Off" (Accompagnement Gratuit)
Problem: Tunisian owners are busy and hate manual data entry. They won't sign up if they have to type 15 cars and 100 clients manually.
Solution: Offer a "We do it for you" service. Tell them: "Send us a video of your paper notebook or list on WhatsApp, and we will import all your cars and clients for you in 1 hour."
Impact: Removes 100% of the friction to start.
Hack 2: WhatsApp PDF Notifications (Alternative to Costly SMS)
Problem: Tunisian SMS packages cost money (0.060 DT per message).
Solution: Integrate a Whatsapp share link or API (e.g. via UltraMsg or simple browser redirect). Let them click "Send on WhatsApp" to send booking confirmations and damage checklists directly to clients.
Impact: Owners save money compared to SMS, and clients love receiving clean, modern WhatsApp updates.
Hack 3: 14-Day Free Trial (No Card Required)
Strategy: Let them sign up using just a phone number, add 2 cars, and create bookings for 14 days for free.
Impact: Once they experience how clean the dashboard is and how easy it makes their daily routine, they won't want to go back to paper notebooks.
Hack 4: Tunisian Invoice & Contract Customization (Timbre Fiscal)
Strategy: Highlight that the system automatically handles the Tunisian stamp tax (Timbre Fiscal) and prints beautiful rental contracts matching local police/legal requirements.
Impact: Instantly builds trust because it feels built specifically for Tunisian law, not a generic American template.
Hack 5: High-Converting Sales Pitch
When calling or visiting agencies, don't sell "software". Sell peace of mind:

"Our system prevents double-bookings, calculates your monthly net profit automatically, alerts you before a car needs oil changes or assurance renewals, and lets you manage everything from your phone while you are away from the office."

2. Risque l-données yitfas5ou w7adhom (Tanthif automatique)?
La, impossible ya7sal hadha! Mafamech "tanthif automatique" yfasakh l-données mte3 l-kraheb walla l-reservations. L-données toq3od tsajla permanent f database.
Mochklet l-Plan Free f Supabase: Ken l-database toq3od 7 ayyam de suite mafiha 7atta activity (ya3ni 7atta owner mayodh7el l-l-site walla yjarreb), Supabase ya3mel Pause l-l-project.
Chnowa ya3ni? L-données matitfasakhch, ama l-site ywalli maymchich l-owners (tatla3 error). Lezmek todkhol l-Supabase dashboard w ta3mel "Unpause" manually bech yarja3 l-site yakhdem.

Risque mte3 overlap mte3 l-données: Hadha tfaqadneh 3la l-kriyya elli fatet. L-Row-Level Security (RLS) mte3 l-database mkhadma s7i7a 100%. Ya3ni Owner A impossible ychouf l-kraheb walla reservations mte3 Owner B, walla ya3mel data leak.
